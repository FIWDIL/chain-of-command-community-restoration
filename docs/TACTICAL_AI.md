# Computer-controlled fireteams

The computer controls both enemy fireteams and unoccupied friendly fireteams. It never changes orders given to your own soldiers.

Fireteams move toward the rally point and react to enemies they can see. Soldiers look for cover, spread out and take turns moving while others provide covering fire. Gunners are usually chosen to cover movement. Smoke can protect an advance, and soldiers wait for it to develop before moving through it.

The computer remembers a recently seen enemy for a short time. It does not keep tracking an enemy that has disappeared. Stressed soldiers prefer nearby cover. Ammunition and grenades are limited for the computer too.

These tactics were written for the restoration. They are not recovered original behavior. Computer decisions still need playtesting, especially around complicated buildings and narrow routes.
